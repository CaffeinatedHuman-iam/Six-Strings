Artists that have influenced Randy

To create the riffs and melodies we have today, Randy drew inspiration from some popular influences and some that might surprise you.

These are some of the Rock Influences that helped shape his style

  • Jimi Hendrix
  • Leslie West (Mountain)
  • Mick Ronson (David Bowie)
  • Michael Schenker (The Scorpions/UFO)
  • Glen Buxton (Alice Cooper)

Classical Influences

  • J.S. Bach
  • Leo Brouwer
  • Francisco Tarrega
  • John Williams
  • Allan Holdsworth
  • Pat Metheny
