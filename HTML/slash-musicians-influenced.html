Musicians Slash Influenced

Some artists are known for their glasses. Slash is known for his top hat, his Les Paul, and his old-school rock sound. These became part of the identity that inspired generations to follow.

Gibson on Slash

Some influences stretch beyond the music. For Slash, his iconic use of the Les Paul inspired generations of players to pick one up themselves.

“Him playing a Les Paul really changed the course of the company in the late 1980s.”
“inspiring multiple generations of players around the world.”
Samantha Fish "Growing up, Slash was one of my original guitar heroes... I love anybody with a personality… so you can just turn a song on and you know exactly who is holding that guitar because they have that personality."
Christone "Kingfish" Ingram "Christone 'Kingfish' Ingram first heard Slash on Guitar Hero... [Ingram recalled] that it was those heavy, melodic rock tracks that first exposed him to that specific style of phrasing before he fully dove into traditional acoustic blues."
Bella Perron (Plush) "My main influences are Randy Rhoads, Joe Satriani, Joe Perry, Nuno Bettencourt, Ace Frehley, Eddie Van Halen, Slash, and Steve Vai. My overall sound and tone is heavily influenced from that era of rock."
Sophie Lloyd“He's one of my biggest inspirations of all time… I couldn't not put a Slash solo in there!"
Mark Tremonti "I don't like watching guitar players that just purely do supernatural fast stuff. I like when people have heart and soul, and he's got a ton of soul,"
Lizzy Hale“Think about it this way – whether you like pop, rap, R&B, anything you feel like you are, everyone always wants to rock... You see it in every generation – people want to wear leather and do stuff with Slash!”
