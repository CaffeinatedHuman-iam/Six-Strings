Artists that have influenced Stevie

To understand this bluesman, you'd have to start at the beginning, where inspiration began for him.

Jimmie Vaughan

"My big brother Jimmie was playing guitar, and I figured I’d try it too. He left his Strat in the house and told his little brother not to touch it, well, I just had to, of course."

Jimi Hendrix

"The first time I ever heard his name was when my brother brought a record of his home in the mid '60s. It was around '67, '68... Jimmie found it in a trash bin behind a record shop."When asked about his approach to covering Hendrix’s music, Stevie added: "There's only one Jimi Hendrix. I’m just trying to play what I feel, but he opened up the doors for everyone.""The first time I ever heard his name was when my brother brought a record of his home in the mid '60s. Albert King

Jimmie Vaughan on Stevie Meeting Albert King

"Albert got Stevie up there, and Stevie commenced doing Albert King licks. There was silence at first. Everyone stood there with their mouths open. They couldn't believe it. But Albert loved it. He put his arm around him, and from then on it was Albert and Stevie. Everybody went, 'Whew, that was scary.' I would never have tried that, but you've got to admire the audacity."

Looking a bit further into what created Stevie, you would find blues, of course, but also a mix of jazz and rock.
