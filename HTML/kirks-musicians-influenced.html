Musicians Kirk Influenced

While Kirk's playing has drawn from many influences over the years, we can't deny how his influence has affected music today.

Synyster Gates (Avenged Sevenfold) "Kirk Hammett's playing combines technical prowess with emotion
and aggression... [He] shaped the thrash metal revolution with finesse."
Mark Holcomb (Periphery) "Every single person who picks up an electric guitar and wants to play heavy music owes a massive debt to Metallica.
Their riffs are the foundation of everything we do today."
Kerry King (Slayer) "When Kirk joined Metallica, it changed the game. His speed and note selection on those early tracks forced everyone in the underground scene to play faster and tighter just to keep up."
Alex Skolnick (Testament) "Kirk's always gonna have a place in my heart because of Metallica, having gotten into them so young way back in the day, and his leads on Ride the Lightning are terrific."
Robb Flynn (Machine Head) "Definitely a thrash influence. When we construct leads, it's always borrowing from Gary Holt or Kirk Hammett, something from that realm."
Corey Beaulieu (Trivium) " I learned to play guitar on Metallica's Seek and Destroy"
