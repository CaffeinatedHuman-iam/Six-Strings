Musicians Stevie Influenced

Some artists are known for their glasses. Slash is known for his top hat, his Les Paul, and his old-school rock sound. These became part of the identity that inspired generations to follow.

John Mayer “I'm a Stevie Ray Vaughan wannabe, because I wanted to be Stevie and I still want to be Stevie. And if you ever pick up a guitar, is there anybody better to want to be than Stevie Ray Vaughan?”
Kenny Wayne Shepherd“Stevie Ray Vaughan was like, you know, a changing point for me. The first time I saw him I was seven years old... and that was a pivotal point in my life. From that point forward, all I wanted to do was to play guitar with that kind of passion and intensity..."
Gary Clark jr“He's a major influence on myself and so many others. You can still walk up and down Sixth Street here in Austin and hear a bunch of young guitarists playing Stevie Ray Vaughan licks.”
Joe Bonamassa "As a child of the '70s, in 1983... there were two guitar players that changed the game for me... The other was Stevie Ray Vaughan, who came out of Austin wielding an old Stratocaster and a Fender amp set to stun, and turned the blues guitar world on its head."
